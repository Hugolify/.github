# Fast, accessible and low-carbon websites. Built on Hugo, edited in the headless CMS of your choice.

Hugolify is an open-source framework that gives you everything you need to launch a professional website quickly: ready-made content types, a rich library of blocks, and integration with the most popular Git-based CMS. No database, no server to maintain, no compromise on performance.

[![Hugolify](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/e74a4e70-7bfd-4f07-8a41-6feb040a013b)](https://www.hugolify.io)

## Why Hugolify?

- ⚡ **Blazing fast**: static pages built with Hugo, the world's fastest static site generator.
- ♿ **Accessible by design**: semantic markup and components built with accessibility in mind from day one.
- 🌱 **Low carbon**: lightweight pages, minimal JavaScript, fewer requests. Good for your visitors, good for the planet.
- 🔍 **Built to be found**: SEO, structured data and AI-readiness included, not bolted on.
- ✍️ **Editor friendly**: your content team edits pages in their favorite CMS, without touching code.
- 🔓 **Open source and sustainable**: no lock-in, no license fees. Your site, your code, your content.

## Everything you need, out of the box

Stop rebuilding the same features on every project.

- **10+ content types**: posts, people, places, jobs, products, publications, real estate and more
- **20+ taxonomies** to organize and connect your content
- **25+ content blocks** to compose rich pages without writing a single line of code

## Built to be found

By search engines and AI assistants alike, with nothing to install.

- **Technical SEO built in**: title and meta description on every page, canonical URLs, Open Graph and Twitter cards with a share image cropped to 1200×630, XML sitemap, robots.txt, per-page `noindex`, and `hreflang` alternates on multilingual sites.
- **Structured data everywhere**: a schema.org JSON-LD graph linked by `@id`, with WebSite, Organization (or LegalService, MedicalClinic, RealEstateAgent…), WebPage and BreadcrumbList, plus the right type for each content: Article, Person, Service, Event, JobPosting, Product, RealEstateListing, FAQPage, VideoObject.
- **Ready for generative engines (GEO)**: `llms.txt` and `llms-full.txt`, plain semantic HTML that crawlers read without running JavaScript, and entities described with expertise, social profiles and awards, so AI assistants understand who you are and what you do.
- **In your editors' hands**: SEO title, description and share image can be overridden on any page, right from the CMS.

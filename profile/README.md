# Fast, accessible and low-carbon websites. Built on Hugo, edited in the headless CMS of your choice.

Hugolify is an open-source framework that gives you everything you need to launch a professional website quickly: ready-made content types, a rich library of blocks, and integration with the most popular Git-based CMS. No database, no server to maintain, no compromise on performance.

## Why Hugolify?

- ⚡ **Blazing fast**: static pages built with Hugo, the world's fastest static site generator.
- ♿ **Accessible by design**: semantic markup and components built with accessibility in mind from day one.
- 🌱 **Low carbon**: lightweight pages, minimal JavaScript, fewer requests. Good for your visitors, good for the planet.
- ✍️ **Editor friendly**: your content team edits pages in their favorite CMS, without touching code.
- 🔓 **Open source and sustainable**: no lock-in, no license fees. Your site, your code, your content.

## Everything you need, out of the box

Stop rebuilding the same features on every project.

- **10+ content types**: posts, people, places, jobs, products, publications, real estate and more
- **20+ taxonomies** to organize and connect your content
- **25+ content blocks** to compose rich pages without writing a single line of code

## Batteries included

Pick your UI foundation and turn on the features your project needs:

| Feature | Powered by |
| --- | --- |
| UI foundation | Design System or Bootstrap |
| Carousels | SplideJS |
| Charts | Chart.js |
| Cookie consent banner | CookieConsent |
| Lightbox | TobiiJS |
| Maps | Leaflet + OpenStreetMap |

## Your CMS, your choice

Hugolify works with 7 headless CMS out of the box. Your content lives in Git, so you can switch at any time.

CloudCannon · Decap CMS · Netlify CMS · Pages CMS · Static CMS · Sveltia CMS · Tina CMS
